About

Connecting genetics with practical wheat improvement

I am a plant geneticist and breeder with training in Genetics & Plant Breeding and research experience across field and glasshouse phenotyping, crossing, quantitative genetics, molecular breeding and transcriptomics.

My current research at Murdoch University focuses on improving nitrogen use efficiency in wheat. My earlier work examined genetic resistance to cereal cyst nematode and root-lesion nematode using GWAS, QTL mapping and transcriptome analysis.

I am particularly interested in research that moves from phenotype to genomic region, candidate gene and breeding application, while keeping field performance at the centre of the question.

Methods & tools

From field data to genomic interpretation

I work across experimental, molecular and computational stages of crop genetics research.

Experimental

Field trials, glasshouse experiments, crossing, generation advancement, phenotyping, DNA/RNA work, PCR and qRT-PCR.

Genetics & genomics

GWAS, QTL mapping, meta-QTL analysis, linkage mapping, haplotypes, LD, population structure, GBS and variant calling.

Analysis
